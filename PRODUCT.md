# Draftly product direction

The [mission](MISSION.md), [vision](VISION.md), and [values](VALUES.md)
describe the purpose and direction of Draftly.

## Audience

Draftly is for people who want a place to write quickly and return to their
words later. It also serves people who want to organize drafts and use actions
to move their writing into other workflows.

## Value

Draftly brings writing, capture, organization, and actions together in a native
Mac app. A person can start with text and choose what to do with it later.

## Priorities

1. Make writing clear, comfortable, and dependable.
2. Make capture and retrieval quick, so people can return to their writing.
3. Offer actions that help people use their words in other workflows.
4. Improve compatibility with Drafts when it supports the writing experience.
5. Explore other devices when they can support that experience well.

These priorities guide decisions; they do not promise a release, feature, or
platform. See [README.md](README.md) for current capabilities and
[CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md) for decision principles.
