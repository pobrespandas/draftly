A little space for whatever comes to mind.

Open a new draft, capture text from anywhere, and let your ideas take shape. Your words save as you work, so you can stay focused on what you’re writing.

From thought to action

Simple when you start. Ready when you need more.

01 / CAPTURE

Get it down

Start a draft with ⌘N, or capture text from another app with a shortcut. The menu bar keeps Capture close at hand.

02 / ORGANIZE

Find your way back

Use tags, flags, search, and saved workspaces to keep your drafts easy to find. Archive what you’re done with.

03 / DO

Put words to work

Run actions, preview Markdown, and revisit earlier versions. Make the editor your own with fonts and themes.
