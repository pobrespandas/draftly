Start with an idea

Start a draft when an idea arrives. Capture text from another app or open Capture from the menu bar.

Give your ideas time and space. Write with an editor you can personalize, preview Markdown, and revisit earlier versions.

Find your way back

Use search, tags, flags, and saved workspaces to return to the drafts you need.

04 / USE

Put words to work

Run actions to copy, share, or export your words and bring them into other workflows.
