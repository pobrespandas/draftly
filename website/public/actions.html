Import an action

  1. Open an action's original page and copy its page URL.
  2. In Draftly for Mac, choose Actions → Import from Directory.
  3. Paste the URL and select Load. Review the action's steps, then select Save Actions.

These are third-party actions. Review their steps and any effects on your drafts, files, or other services before running them.
