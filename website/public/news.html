October 31, 2026Draftly 0.1.0

The first public release

Draftly 0.1.0 brings writing, capture, organization, and actions together in a native Mac app.

  • Capture and organize drafts with tags, flags, and Workspaces.
  • Edit plain text, Markdown, HTML, MultiMarkdown, and TaskPaper.
  • Use Actions to transform, share, and export your writing.
  • Export and restore full library backups.
