# Values

- **Care for writing:** Put writing at the center of Draftly. Care for the
  space, tools, and details that help people think and develop ideas.
- **Flexibility:** Adapt to different ways of writing and working, from a
  quick draft to text that develops over time.
- **Trust:** Care for people's work. Help them return to it, understand its
  status, and keep control of their data.
- **Clarity:** Make features, their effects, and their limits easy to understand.
- **Autonomy:** Let people decide how to organize, transform, and use their
  words. Provide tools without requiring one way of working.
- **Simplicity:** Make it easy to start and focus on what matters. Make advanced
  features available when people need them.

See [CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md) for how these values guide decisions.
