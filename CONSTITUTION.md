# Draftly constitution

These principles guide product, design, and engineering decisions for the app
and its website. They are revisable as Draftly evolves. They apply the
[values](VALUES.md) to decisions and support the direction in
[PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

## Put writing first

The writing experience is the central purpose of Draftly. Choose features and
interface changes that help people capture, develop, and use their ideas. When
a feature adds complexity, keep the basic writing path clear and make advanced
options available when needed.

## Make capture easy

Let people start writing with few steps. Keep capture paths quick and preserve
the option to organize or act on text later.

## Support different workflows

Give people choices for how they write, organize, and use their work. Support
connections to other workflows without requiring a single sequence of steps.

## Care for people's data

Protect drafts and respect the choices people make about their data. Favor
recoverable operations, clear errors, and ways to export or back up work.
Describe storage and synchronization according to the evidence available.

## Be clear about capabilities

Describe what works, what is still in development, and what needs verification.
Use the same standard in the app, website, and documentation. Do not present a
planned capability as an available feature.

## Feel at home on each platform

Use platform conventions when they help people understand and control the app.
On Mac, support native interaction patterns and accessibility. Extend to other
platforms only when the writing experience can meet the same care.

## Make automation useful and understandable

Offer actions that help people use their writing. Make their effects clear and
give people control over consequential operations. Compatibility with Drafts
supports this goal; it does not override the writing experience.

## Design for change

Give components clear responsibilities and interfaces. Keep related rules
together, hide internal details, and limit dependencies. Separate domain,
storage, execution, integrations, and interface concerns where practical.
Choose the simplest design that supports the change and preserves behavior.
